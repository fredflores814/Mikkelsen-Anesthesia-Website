MAC & IV Sedation

IV sedation and monitored anesthesia care (MAC) keep you comfortable and pain-free while you breathe on your own — ideal for most in-office dental and outpatient procedures.

General Anesthesia

For complex or lengthy procedures, general anesthesia provides complete unconsciousness and pain control. Delivered by IV or inhalation and managed safely in your office with full monitoring and recovery oversight.

Regional Anesthesia

Regional anesthesia numbs a specific region of the body — such as an arm or leg — while you remain awake and comfortable. It offers excellent pain control for longer procedures and is well suited to mobile, office-based care.

Local Anesthesia

Local anesthesia numbs a small, targeted area — like a tooth or section of skin — so treatment can be completed comfortably while you stay awake.

Pediatric Anesthesia

Children need specialized anesthesia care. My pediatric services keep young patients safe, comfortable, and calm during dental and outpatient procedures — backed by experienced CRNAs, hospital-grade monitoring, and family-focused communication across Wisconsin and Northern Illinois.

Specialized Care Services

High-Anxiety & Trauma-Informed Anesthesia Care

Severe dental anxiety, past traumatic experiences, or difficulty tolerating treatment often call for more than standard sedation. I provide office-based IV sedation and general anesthesia designed for high-anxiety patients, helping dental and oral surgery practices manage complex cases safely with predictable workflows and monitoring standards.

Special Needs & Neurodiverse Anesthesia Care

Patients with developmental disabilities, sensory sensitivities, or complex medical needs need anesthesia plans built around them. I partner with dental and oral surgery practices to deliver individualized, office-based sedation and general anesthesia — hospital-level monitoring in a familiar clinical setting.
